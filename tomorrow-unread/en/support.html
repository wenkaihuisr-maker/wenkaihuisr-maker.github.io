TOMORROW UNREAD / SUPPORT

Investigator’s Guide and Support

Where do I begin?

Read tomorrow’s record, then explore Inbox and Evidence. Open a document and tap “Collect Evidence,” then choose an action in Deduce. Reading a conversation does not count as collecting evidence.

Missing evidence or a wrong deduction

Deduce tells you which evidence is missing. Return to Evidence to collect it, or open a hint. Wrong deductions carry no score penalty and can be retried.

Language and reading

In Help, open “Language / 语言” and choose English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, or the system option. Your selection is saved on this device and is not sent to the developer. Reading text follows your system text-size preferences.

Progress and restarting

Progress is saved automatically on your device. To start over, choose Restart Case in Help and confirm; the previous save is backed up. If a save cannot be read, use the app’s recovery screen instead of repeatedly deleting the app. If storage is full, free some space before retrying the save.

Endings and sharing

After completing the case, revisit the final choice to explore another tomorrow. Preview your case card, choose whether to reveal the ending, and use the system share sheet to share or save the image. Canceling a share does not change your progress.

Get help

Submit a public support request and include “Tomorrow Unread” in the title. Describe the app version, iOS version, device model, steps, expected result, and actual result. Do not upload account details, private photos, save files, or other sensitive information. This channel requires a GitHub account, and submissions are public.

The app has no login, in-app purchases, or subscriptions, so there are no purchases to restore.
