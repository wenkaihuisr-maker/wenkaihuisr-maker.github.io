TOMORROW UNREAD / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026 · Developer: kaihui wen

Data in the game

Tomorrow Unread requires no account. Its characters, messages, and documents are fictional. The app does not access real chats, contacts, photos, or location. It has no advertising, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server, and does not send game progress to the developer.

Local progress, preferences, and backups

The app stores the investigation stage, collected clues, choice history, and ending in its own storage on your device. App preferences record whether you have started playing and your language selection, including whether to follow the system language. These preferences remain on the device and are not sent to the developer. Before restarting, the app keeps a backup of the previous save to avoid accidental loss. App data may be included in Apple device backups if you have enabled them; this is controlled by the system and your settings.

Sharing you choose

Only when you open the share sheet and choose a destination does the system pass the case-card image to your selected app or save location. Cards hide the ending by default, with an option to reveal it. The recipient handles the content under its own policy. The app does not read your photo library.

Deleting data

In iPhone Storage in iOS Settings, select this app and choose “Delete App,” not “Offload App,” to remove local app data, preferences, and old save backups. Images you have shared or saved elsewhere must be deleted separately at those destinations. Copies in Apple system backups are managed through your backup settings. The developer holds no server-side copy of your progress.

Support and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting service may process request information such as your IP address to serve pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no advertising, analytics scripts, or forms to this site. Support requests you choose to submit are public on GitHub; do not include private information or save files.

Contact and updates

For questions, use the developer’s public support channel and mention “Tomorrow Unread.” Policy updates will show a new effective date on this page.
