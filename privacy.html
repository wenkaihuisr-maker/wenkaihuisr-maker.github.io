Privacy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Local storage
Projects, scripts, reference images, and captured media are stored on the device. WrapSure has no account service, analytics, ads, or tracking.
Camera & Microphone
Permission is requested only when recording. Denial leaves planning and Files or Photos import alternatives available.
Photos & Files
Access is requested only for the import or export action you choose. Files uses the system document picker. WrapSure does not scan unrelated libraries or folders.
StoreKit
Apple processes Creator Lifetime purchase and restore. The app reads verified entitlement state and does not receive payment-card details.
Sharing
Data leaves the device only when you explicitly export or share a backup, PDF, CSV, or media.
Retention & Deletion
Content remains until you delete a project, remove the app according to device behavior, or manage exported copies at their destinations.
Children
WrapSure is a general creative tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information.
Policy changes
Material changes will update the effective date and the eventual authoritative public notice before they take effect.
Contact
For privacy or support questions, open a public support request. Do not include private recordings, financial information, passwords, or other sensitive data.

隐私说明

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 1 日

本机存储
项目、脚本、参考图和拍摄媒体保留在设备上；无账户服务、分析、广告或跟踪。
相机与麦克风
只在录制时请求权限。拒绝后仍可规划，并可选择照片或文件导入。
照片与文件
只为你选择的导入或导出操作请求访问，不扫描无关图库或目录。
StoreKit
Apple 处理 Creator Lifetime 购买与恢复；应用只读取经验证的权益状态。
保留与删除
内容保留至你删除项目或管理导出副本。卸载时按设备系统行为处理。
儿童
WrapSure 是通用创作工具，不面向儿童，也不会故意收集个人信息。
政策变更
重要变更生效前，最终权威公开说明将更新生效日期与内容。
联系
如有隐私或支持问题，请提交公开支持请求。请勿提交私人录制、财务信息、密码或其他敏感数据。
